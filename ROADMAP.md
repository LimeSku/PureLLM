# Roadmap

1. Add greedy, top-k, and top-p generation.
2. Add a KV cache and compare generation speed.
3. Compare architecture choices: positional encodings, normalization,
   activations, and attention variants.
4. Compare training choices: optimizers, learning-rate schedules, gradient
   accumulation, and mixed precision.
5. Explore character and BPE tokenization with different vocabulary sizes.
6. Add conversational fine-tuning, assistant-only loss, and LoRA.
7. Visualize attention, embeddings, activations, and gradients.
8. Experiment with compilation, quantization, and distributed training.
